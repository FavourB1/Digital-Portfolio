# Digital-Portfolio
## Here, you will see my interactive designs and User Experience sketches, wireframes or prototypes...Who knows what more?
 
I am a Ph.D. student and digital experience strategist operating at the critical intersection of media, art, and technology, with a scholarly focus on **Human-Computer Interaction (HCI)** and **User Experience (UX)**. 

My research is dedicated to analyzing how the integration of **Generative AI (GenAI)** is  reshaping the UX design process, moving designers from traditional technical roles to roles requiring critical collaboration with the machine. 

This investigation is driven by a deep concern for the ethical and social implications of this shift, specifically analyzing how varying **digital literacy levels** influence the usability and equity of AI-generated designs. Leveraging a robust methodology that includes **UX Research**, and **critical making**, and grounded in theoretical frameworks like **Design Justice**, my work aims to provide actionable, human-centered insights for the next generation of designers, ensuring that future digital products are both innovative and globally inclusive.
